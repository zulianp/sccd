\subsection{Contributions and structure of the paper}
\label{sec:contribution}

We present SCCD, a two-phase CCD pipeline for triangle \emph{and} quad meshes,
with host and device implementations of both phases. Its conservativeness is
structural and not empirical (\cref{sec:guarantee}), and we assess it against
TightInclusion on a dataset whose roots are known exactly. The contributions of
this paper are the following:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item a broad phase with no tuning parameter and no sort, built on a
        two-dimensional uniform cell list whose cell size is derived from the
        data, with one query per pair type;
  \item an order-independence result for the underlying branch-and-bound
        search, which licenses vector lanes and thread blocks to be filled with
        work from unrelated queries;
  \item a host narrow phase that packs SIMD lanes across queries, and a device
        narrow phase that redistributes work through a double-buffered global
        queue;
  \item a signed accuracy assessment against a certified reference over
        $11{,}630{,}064$ checks against exact symbolic roots, on two processors
        and six scenes;
  \item a performance assessment against two further implementations, on the
        question each of them answers.
\end{enumerate}

The first contribution is a broad phase whose two queries have different shapes
and are constructed differently. The vertex-face query bins a box into every
cell its extent touches and attributes a pair to the cell holding the corner of
the overlap (\cref{sec:bp:cell}). The edge-edge query is one list against
itself, which admits one entry per box at its minimum corner and a forward walk
in row-major cell order, pruned by a prefix maximum per row of cells, so that no
bound on the number of cells a box may span is required (\cref{sec:bp:self}).

The search of TightInclusion is a priority queue ordered by the time lower
bound, and its answer is the first box it accepts. We show in
\cref{sec:np:order} that keeping the minimum over \emph{all} accepted boxes and
pruning against it yields the same answer under \emph{any} traversal order. On
the host this licenses a lane to hold boxes from different queries
(\cref{alg:host}), turning a search with per-query divergence into a vector
kernel, and sibling boxes then inherit four of their eight corner values from
their parent, halving the dominant cost.

On the device the same freedom is spent on redistribution. The difficulty of a
CCD query is heavy-tailed: on one scene most queries need a single box, while
$208$ of them need more than a million (\cref{tab:difficulty}). A large
per-block work stack keeps such a query inside the block that seeded it while
its neighbours idle. We therefore use a deliberately \emph{small} shared stack
that spills early into a double-buffered global queue, which the next launch
redistributes across every block (\cref{alg:device}). On the worst scene this is
worth two orders of magnitude (\cref{tab:stackcap}).

The accuracy assessment covers $11{,}630{,}064$ query-mode checks against exact
symbolic roots on both processors and all six scenes, of which $11{,}044{,}766$
place a reported time of impact beside an exact root. SCCD reports no missed
collision and no late time of impact, and reproduces TightInclusion's median and
worst-case error to every printed digit while running faster
(\cref{sec:results:ref}).

We also report the configurations on which the pipeline is the slower of the
two, and the reasons. The device narrow phase is slower than the host on two of
the six scenes, for a structural reason we identify, and on both of them this
places the whole pipeline behind the host. The host cell list leads the device
broad phase up to roughly six million elements, above which the ordering
reverses. The mode that trades accuracy for speed is \emph{slower} on four of
the six host scenes.

The remainder of this paper is organised as follows.
\Cref{sec:prelim} states the three queries, the difference function whose roots
they reduce to, and the certified error bound that makes a floating-point
decision about those roots sound. \Cref{sec:bp} describes the broad phase and
its two queries, and \cref{sec:np} the narrow-phase search, its two acceptance
tests and the order-independence result. \Cref{sec:parallel} gives the host and
device implementations of that search and the measurements that explain how the
work divides between the two processors. \Cref{sec:guarantee} assembles the
conservativeness argument and identifies where an implementation can break it.
\Cref{sec:results} reports the experiments: the machine and the dataset, the
conservativeness and accuracy assessments against TightInclusion, and the
performance assessment against Scalable CCD and additive CCD.
\Cref{sec:limitations} collects the limitations and the threats to validity,
\cref{sec:availability} the software and data, and \cref{sec:conclusion}
concludes.
