\subsection{Contributions and structure of the paper}
\label{sec:contribution}

We present SCCD, a two-phase CCD pipeline for triangle \emph{and} quad meshes,
with host and device implementations of both phases. Its conservativeness is
structural, inherited from the certified bound of \citet{wang2021tight}
(\cref{sec:np}), and we assess it against TightInclusion on a dataset whose
roots are known exactly. The contributions of this paper are the following:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item a broad phase with no tuning parameter and no sort, built on a
        two-dimensional uniform cell list whose cell size is derived from the
        data and which keeps one entry per box, at its minimum corner
        (\cref{sec:bp:grid}): cumulative maxima per cell and per row bound how
        far a query must read back, so the edge-edge query walks forward from
        its own cell in row-major order (\cref{sec:bp:self}) and the vertex-face
        query a range those maxima close from below (\cref{sec:bp:cell});
  \item an order-independence result for the underlying branch-and-bound search
        (\cref{sec:np:order}): keeping the minimum over \emph{all} accepted
        boxes and pruning against it yields the same answer under \emph{any}
        traversal order, which licenses vector lanes and thread blocks to be
        filled with work from unrelated queries;
  \item two parallel narrow phases that spend that freedom in opposite
        directions (\cref{sec:parallel}). The host packs SIMD lanes across
        queries (\cref{alg:host}), so sibling boxes inherit four of their eight
        corner values from their parent. The device pairs a deliberately
        \emph{small} per-block stack with a double-buffered global queue that
        the next launch redistributes (\cref{alg:device}), which matters
        because query difficulty is heavy-tailed: on one scene seven queries in
        ten are settled by a single box and the worst needs more than four
        million (\cref{tab:difficulty});
  \item a signed accuracy assessment against a certified reference over
        $11{,}630{,}064$ checks against exact symbolic roots, on two processors
        and six scenes, of which $11{,}044{,}766$ place a reported time of
        impact beside an exact root. SCCD reports no missed collision and no
        late time of impact, and reproduces TightInclusion's median and
        worst-case error to every printed digit while running faster
        (\cref{sec:results:ref});
  \item a performance assessment against two further implementations, each on
        the question it answers (\cref{sec:results:others}).
\end{enumerate}

We also report the configurations on which the pipeline is the slower of the
two. The device narrow phase is slower than the host on two of the six scenes,
for a structural reason we identify, and on both of them this places the whole
pipeline behind the host. The host cell list leads the device broad phase up to
roughly six million elements, above which the ordering reverses.

The remainder of this paper is organised as follows. \Cref{sec:prelim} states
the three queries, the difference function whose roots they reduce to, and the
certified error bound that makes a floating-point decision about those roots
sound. \Cref{sec:bp} describes the broad phase and \cref{sec:np} the
narrow-phase search. \Cref{sec:parallel} gives the two parallel realisations.
\Cref{sec:results} reports the experiments and \cref{sec:availability} the
software and data.
